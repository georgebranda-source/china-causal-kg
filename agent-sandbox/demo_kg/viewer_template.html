China Mail, Hongkong · 16 June – 3 July 1925

What caused the 1925 Hongkong strike, according to the China Mail

A proof-of-concept causal knowledge graph built from OCR'd pages of a British colonial newspaper. Each arrow is a causal claim made in print. Click any arrow to read the original 1925 sentences behind it, with who made the claim.

explicit implicit counterfactual thicker = more articles

How it was built

  1. Extraction. Every quote is checked by script to appear word for word in the OCR text, so OCR errors are kept as printed.
  2. Clustering.
  3. Review.
  4. Graph. Relations between the same two events are merged into one arrow that keeps every quote as evidence.

How to read it

Arrows record what the paper or its sources claimed, not established causes. The China Mail was a British colonial paper, so instigation by Canton, Moscow or "agitators" is heavily represented, and the strikers' own reasons mostly reach print through quoted officials or a union secretary.

Contested claims stay separate. The Shameen shooting of 23 June has two incoming arrows that contradict each other: one from the Canton Christian College staff statement, one from its retraction.

Limits of this demo

Extraction and review here were done by Claude as a stand-in for the planned local LLM (Llama or Mistral), and nobody has hand-checked them yet. The corpus is of the 40 strike-related pages already transcribed. Many events appear only once, so the graph is fragmented. More pages should join these fragments into longer chains.
