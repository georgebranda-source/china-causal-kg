## [4] WAR DEBTS.

It transpires that the United States took the initiative in seeking a general refunding arrangement with all America's principal war debtors. It is indicated that the United States Government virtually circularised the debtors, including France, Italy, Belgium, Czecho-Slovakia, Greece, Jugo, Slavia, Estonia and Latvia, expressing the opinion that the time had come to strike a general balance. Russia is the only debtor nation not addressed, as she does not possess a Government recognised by the United States. Furthermore, the French Government has been informed that the United States will be pleased if a French Debt Commission is sent to America. It is possible that M. ## [7] POCR AIM.

**MOROCCO TRIBESMEN'S GUNS.**

*PARIS, May 17.*

A message from Fez states that General Freydenberg's forces have relieved the Bou Toument post after severe fighting in the course of which the enemy used two guns of which the fire was badly directed.

Enemy propaganda is intensifying on the Branes and Ghennals fronts to the east. The tribesmen on the Ghennals front are reported to have been reinforced by 700 rifles.

## [8] SOVIET INJUSTRY.

*(Router's Service.)*

Moscow, May 16.
Replying to some opposition from the industrial districts expressed at the Congress of Soviets to the scheme to increase imports, M. Dzjerjinsky, chairman of the All Russian Council of National Economies, pointed out that increase of machinery was unavoidable in order to effect the quickest possible revival of industry, whose production was approaching the pre-war figure. The peasants' demand for agricultural implements had increased to such a degree that the Soviet factories were unable to satisfy it. Seventeen million roubles worth of agricultural implements must be imported during the current year.

The Hague, May 17.—The Permanent Court's ruling in connection with the Danzig letter-boxes gives the opinion that no ruling was made in connection with the disputed point by the Danzig High Commissioner, but the Polish postal service of the port is entitled to install letter-boxes outside its premises, though the service is not reserved for Polish officials and authorities but is for the public use.—Refer.

London, May 16.—It is officially announced that the attendance at Wembley cup to Friday night was 350,000, as compared with 302,000 for the first six days last year, the latter including Cup Final day. Therefore it is evident that the attendance has this year hitherto greatly-increased.—Reuter.

## [10] IMPRESSIVE CEREMONY.

**POPE SPEAKS INTO LOUD**

*(Router's Service.)*

*Rome, May 17:*

An impressive ceremony exceeding six hours was carried out by the Pope on the occasion of the first of the Holy Year canonisations which will be performed every Sunday for the next few weeks.

The French nun, Sister Therese, of Child Jesus, was to-day elevated into sainthood in the presence of 60,000 people.
An electric loud speaker operated for the first time inside St. Peter's, carried the Pontiff's voice to the most distant corners of the Basilica.

The dome, facade and obelisk of St. Peter's were illuminated for the first time since 1870.

## [11] CHEUNG CHAU

The Kung Yik has been back on the run for a week and looks spick and span but we wonder when her next visit to dock will take place. We hear that the Cheung Chau is having her overhaul.

To-day a start was made by the Botanical Dept. in digging the holes for tree planting on the Hall site. A new path to the front entrance has been laid out and in a few years we hope to have a row of trees on either side. Further garden improvements are being carried out at No. 3.

Further contingents of visitors from Indo-China have arrived, others are expected shortly.

The new shops on the Bund are having the roofs put on; a two storey house towards the west of the village is nearing completion. I have it on good authority that a further five shops are soon to be commenced on the Bund. Most of the trees have now got their Spring dresses and in a few weeks the Island will be looking its best; the wild fowering shrubs are commencing to bloom.

No further move has been made in regard to the Wireless telephone or the repair of the Pier; both are urgently needed.

The Compradore who opened up the foreign store here last year did not meet with sufficient encouragement to warrant his repeating the experiment this year, however some other man with a Hongkong connection may see his way to serve us in this way.

## [12] HOME FOR WAIFS.

Stanley peninsula may not be chosen as the site of the proposed Home for Waifs and Strays as a result of the visit of the promoters of the scheme to the industrial home at Macao.

The extensiveness and general lay-out of the Macao institution particularly impressed the visitors, and although they have not definitely decided to abandon the present proposed site, it is their intention to approach the Government with a view to finding, if possible, a larger site capable of easy development.

Berlin, May 16.—The Reichstag Budget Committee adonted the Presidential estimates, providing for an increase in the President's annual salary to 60,000 marks, and expenses to 120,000 marks. The Communists opposed the proposal. The Socialists abstained from voting.—Reuter.

## [16] BOXING PROGRESS.

**HONGKONG MAN'S SUCCESS AT HOME.**

Hongkong boxing "fans" who saw A. B. Willoughby, formerly of H.M.S. Hawkins, in action, will be pleased to hear of his progress in the Old Country.

He has been matched with Marine Trinder in an eliminating contest, the winner to meet the winner of the Tom Berry v. Phil Scott contest for the cruiser-weight championship of England, which is now held by Berry. It will thus be seen that Willoughby has moved up to the top ranks and his progress provides yet another instance of servicemen being able to hold their own with the best of professionals.
