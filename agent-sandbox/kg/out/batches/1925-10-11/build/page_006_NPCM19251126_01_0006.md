## [8] MEMORIAL SERVICE

**FUNERAL OF THE QUHEN MOTHER.**

**FRIDAY'S OBSERVANCH**

A "Government Gazette Extraordinary" issued yesterday announces.—

"Whereas the day of the funeral of Her late Majesty Queen Alexandra is to be one of Public Mourning," His Excellency, the Governor in Council directs that Friday, November 27, 1935, shall be observed in this Colony as a dies non, under and with the effect mentioned in section 7 of Ordinance No. 5 of 1912.
A memorial service will be held in St. John's Cathedral at 10 o'clock to which all classes of the community are invited. A limited number of seats will be reserved for the Legislative Council, Consuls, Navy and Army Officers, Government Officials, heads of business houses and soldiers and sailors. The North Transept, Chapel, Baptistry and parts of the Navy and side circles will be open to the general public. The seating capacity of the Cathedral is 700 with additional standing room for about

capacity of the Cathedral is 700 with additional standing room for about 300.

The congregation is asked to be seated by 9:15 a.m. Music will be played at 9:50.

The service will last about 40 minutes. There will be no sermon.

At St. Andrew's Church, Kowloon, a memorial service will be held at 10 a.m. to tomorrow and the Rev. J. Horace Johnston, of Union Church, will take part.

A special memorial service will be held by the Parsee Community of Hongkong to-morrow at the Parsee Church when the head priest will hold a Jason ceermony and recite prayers and invocations in memory of the late Queen Mother. In these the Parsee Community will join.

A Chinese student who was driving a motor car along Nathan Road collided with a public ricsha near St. Andrew's Church.
