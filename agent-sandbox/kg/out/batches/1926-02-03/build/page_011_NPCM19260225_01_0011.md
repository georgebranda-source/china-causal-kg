## [8] *(no headline: column 3)*

Capital $50,000 (Hongkong Currency) divided into five thousand shares of $10 each, of which four thousand are now offered for public subscription, payable $2.50 on application, and $7.50 on allotment.

This Company is being formed for the purpose of taking over the lucrative business hitherto carried on by various private concerns with their head offices on Mount Hewitt. In Bias Bay.

All the concerns which are being taken over have recently been doing a most lucrative business. It is estimated that the expenses of the new Company will be extremely low, and the profits extremely high. Apart from allowances to the dependants of any of the Company's employees who may fall into the hands of the Shanghai Mixed Courts, no salaries are payable, the employees all being perfectly satisfied to work upon a commission basis. The amalgamation of the present private enterprises will eliminate overlapping, and the waste caused by rival institutions covering the same ground (or water). With the recent increase in business, and the proposed amalgamation, it is reasonable to expect enormous expansion in the future. The travelling population (foreigners and Chinese) is constantly increasing, as also is the movement of bullion and other valuables by sea, and no fulling-off is to be expected while the Government Railways remain in the hands of the Militarists.

The services of the most experienced of the Bias Bay Chiefs have been engaged as Managing Director, and the Board will include a number of Bolshevik agitators, Kuomintang extremists, and Tuchuns, all of whom can be relied upon to do their utmost to further the objects of the new Company.
The books of the two principal private companies for the past three months, show that the total turnover amounted to $110,000 in cash, in addition to large quantities of valuables, including silks, furs, watches, jewellery, etc. These form the proceeds of the seizures of the ships "Tungchow," and "Jade." After making liberal provision for commission for the Company's employees, and for the reserve fund for the pensioning of orphans and widows of deceased pirates (whether they meet with a violent death, or succumb from natural causes) it is estimated that these two venturer alone produced a net profit of $50,000. The Directors are confident that even more lucrative business can be done in the near future, and that the economies
affected by the amalgamation of competing interests will produce an even higher percentage of profit on every venture. The assets of the new Company consist of goodwill, which is valued at $10,000; from the ammunition stocks ($10,000); opium store, $9,000; one Chinese gunboat book value $17,500; and Pension Fund $3,500.

## [9] *(no headline: column 4)*

In addition to the Board of Directors an Advisory Committee has been formed, which will include Comrade Borodin (who is an expert on the subject of China's Sovereign Rights); a Prominent Missionary (yet to be selected) who is convinced that everything that China does is right, and everything that any foreigner does is wrong; the Chairman of the local Students' Union through whom arrangements can be made for the disposal of British or Japanese goods which might otherwise be boycotted; and a Brazilian Chinese, who will manage the Company's Bias Bay Casino.

In addition to piracy, ordinary and extraordinary, the Company will as opportunity offers, engage in other potentially lucrative business. It intends, for instance, to promote a system of anti-piracy insurance under which payment of a substantial fee will render the persons or property insured immune from search or seizure. Holders of these special Bias Bay policies, even if they find themselves on a pirated vessel, will be treated with every possible consideration. It is confidently expected that Foreign and Chinese Banks will avail themselves of these policies for the protection of shipments of bullion.

The Company also believes that there is lucrative business to be done in kidnapping and in the sale of arms and opium.

Part of the new Capital will be used to erect a commodious Hostel for the accommodation of kidnapped passengers, equipped with a modern crematorium and other conveniences.

Being incorporated in Hongkong the Company comes under British protection, and will have nothing to fear from the British Navy.

Shareholders will receive a monthly list of seizures by the Company's employees together with a price-list of articles for sale, which will be marked down to far below their market value. Registered shareholders will perpetually enjoy all the immunities in respect both of their persons and properties, which are granted under the Bias Bay Insurance scheme.
The Company has obtained the services of a fuel expert, who will report from time to time upon the suitability of various kinds of cargo as a substitute for coal, in the event of the bunkers running low.

The following documents may be inspected in the original, at the Company's temporary Head Office:

Agreements between the Suian, Tungchow, and Jade Piracy Companies transferring all their interests, goodwill, plant, etc. to the new Company.

Articles of Association: Agreements with Comrade Borodin, the aggressive Missionary, the Student Leader, and the Brazilian Chinese, already referred to:
Proposed forms of protest against the infringement of China's Sovereign rights in Bias Bay, to be used if and when required:
Proposed form of Bias Bay Insurance Policy
In the event of any person applying for more shares than can be allotted any excess payment upon application over shares allotted shall be returned to such person as soon as possible, without interest.
Further information regarding the Company may be obtained, and certified copies of the above documents may be seen, on application to our local representative, Mr. Talkoo F. Isher, Tungchow Road, Tientsin.
