## [20] WOMAN TO USURP MAN'S PRIVITECHS?

**COURTESY AS INSULT!**

The alleged "unrest" among women, which signified a spirit of discontent and refractoriness, was noticeable some years before the war, since when a marked change has taken place, both remarkable and astonishing in its effect upon the nation's commerce and prosperity, (writes A. Leonard Summers, in the "Sunday Express").

What amounted to a desire for "advancement" of the feminine sex, so emphatically initiated by the suffragette movement, has gradually developed into an upheaval, almost a sexual revolution: The tastes, dress, manners, customs, ambitions, emotions, life, and psychology have practically reversed. Football, boxing, gabmling, and all other sports, hitherto the monopoly of the male sex, are now generally adopted everywhere in this country by women, who now openly profess to regard refinement and courtesy as signs of weakness.

Indeed, one polite gentleman, who foolishly imagined that the "fair" sex still appreciated courtesy, was bluntly told by a "lady" that it was not wanted, and that she considered courtesy towards her sex was an insult!

## [21] REVERSING THE SEXES.

There is a tendency to ape male styles as regards dress in every detail, so that from appearance and deportment it is very difficult to distinguish a female from a male. Add to all this the constant penetration by women into men's spheres of activity and it becomes apparent that the ambition of the female is to usurp male privileges.

If we inquire into the masculine idio syncrasies we shall perceive a severe contrast, clearly revealing the other great change astounding contention that the sexes of his generation are undergoing a startling reversing process.

Our young men appear to be becoming almost mentally flabby; their apathy is appalling. Nothing but the eternal "sport" matters; industry and trade may go to the dogs so long as there is football and racing. Abnormal taxation, bureaucratic tyranny, unemployment, diluted beer (at high prices) — nothing rouses their resentment, it seems, whereas the mere suggestion of taxing or tampering with the Briton's been caused riots thirty years

What is the caus eof this extraordinary change, this deadening indifference? For some considerable time the young Englishman has been growing more and more effeminate—first observed when he began to decorate himself with rings, bracelets, etc., and bestowed undue care and attention upon his oily hair, brushing it back in what was called the "knut" fashion.

## [22] FAINTING MEN.

Fashionable barbers declare that the modern young man spends hours at their shops, disbursing anything from three shillings to as many pounds on this wonderful make-up; That he is highly emotional we learn from the London Grand Guignol management, who stated that their "thrills" were often too much for his delicate nerves, causing fainting. "Of every ten persons who are too overcome to remain in the theatre, nine are men," we are told.

This unsatisfactory situation is distinctly bad for the trade, industry and progress of this country, and in my opinion the root cause of it is the feminine influence at work in our educational system. A schoolmaster says: "Several thousands of women are to-day teaching in boys' schools, with the result that 700,000 boys are being 'educated' by women. The teaching profession has been allowed to become the experimenting ground for the feminist movement of sex squabbles."

The present system is unnatural, obviously. Men should train the male mind, if there is to be strength of character, breadth of vision, courage, organisation, sound judgment, and other qualifications with which we expect men to be equipped.
