A book, was produced by Mr. M. W. Lo (for the defendant) in support of the claim that the plaintiff went on board the "Empress of Canada" collecting the individual debts from the boys and jotting down their names and the amounts.

In view of this Mr. Justice Wood said that he did not think it much good proceeding with the case.
Mr. L. D. Turner (for the plaintiff) concurred. He had evidently been misinformed by his client and had no option but to drop the case.

Judgment was entered for the defendant with costs.
