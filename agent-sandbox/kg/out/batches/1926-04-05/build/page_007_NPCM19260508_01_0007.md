## [6] ISSUE CHANGED.

### Political and Constitutional.

The "British Gazette" contends that the fact that the "British Worker" is published entirely by Trade Unionists proves that the strike is purely political and aimed at the newspapers whose opinions the Trade Union Congress disapproves.

It states that apart from the miners the strikers do not exceed a million of whom vast numbers were ordered to strike without being consulted.

The issue has ceased to be industrial and has become political and constitutional.

## [11] STEEL TRUST.

**NOW OFFICIALLY EXISTS IN GERMANY.**

*(Reuter's Service.)*

*Berlin, May 7*

The Rhenish-Westphalian Steel Trust officially exists to-day and is known as the Vereinigte Stahlwerke Ekiengesellschaft. It formally took over the Phoenix, Rheinstahl, Gelsenkirchen, Bochumverein, Deutsch-Luxemburg, Van Der Zypenwissen and Thyssen concerns. The managing director is Dr. Voegler. The capital is eight hundred million marks.

## [12] WORK OF LEAGUE OF NATIONS

**A DESERVED TRIBUTE.**

The League of Nations Health Committee has adopted the second part of the Medical Director's report on his voyage to the Far East relating to the work of the Singapore Epidemiological and Intelligence Bureau, and passed a number of resolutions concerning, interalia, the development of the League's epidemiological service and the establishment of close contact with the Japanese public health service activities.

The report pays a tribute to the notable services of the Bureau to Eastern health administration, especially in having kept them promptly informed in regard to a serious cholera outbreak in several Asiatic ports during the past twelve months.
