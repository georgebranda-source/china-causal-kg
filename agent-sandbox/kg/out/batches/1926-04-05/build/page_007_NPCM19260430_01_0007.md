## [2] French Debt.

<sub>section under [1] WAR DEBTS:</sub>

Washington, April 29: A settlement has been reached for the funding of the French debt. Terms. Detailed.

The settlement is on a basis of 62 years, with the payment of $30,000,000 annually for the first two years, $82,500,000 annually for the next two years, and graduating thereafter to a maximum of $125,000,000 annually.

It is calculated that the payments will aggregate $6,847,000,000. No interest will charged for the first five years, but thereafter the rate of interest will gradually increase from one per cent. in the first 10 years to 3½ per cent. in the last 22 years.

There is said to be no safety clause.

## [11] Byrd's Expectations.

<sub>section under [10] POLAR RACE.</sub>

The Chantier, which has a speed of nearly ten knots, besides carrying the two planes, has all the other equipment required for the Polaris expedition. Her hold is filled with coal, and a cargo of 4,500 gallons of airplane fuel will be taken along. Food supplies for six months will be carried. The fuel supply is expected to permit approximately 10,000 miles of flying. The most modern navigational equipment will be installed in the two planes Byrd will use. The sun compass, which was used by Byrd and MacMillan with success on the expedition last year, has been improved during the winter, and an earth induction compass, a highly sensitive instrument, will be carried in the planes.

## [12] The Base.

<sub>section under [10] POLAR RACE.</sub>

Commander Byrd is confident that a landing can be made at Cape Morris. Jesup during May. During this period everything is covered with snow, which he regards as safer than ice formations. Though the cold is more intense during May, the better wind and fog conditions will compensate for this drawback. All flights from the base at Cape Morris Jesup will carry the explorers over territory which is unexplored and if conditions are not such as will permit flights to the Pole, explorations can be made in this region, which has never been seen by man.

## [13] Radio Experts:

<sub>section under [10] POLAR RACE.</sub>

Commander Byrd expects to keep in constant radio communication with his base ship at King's Bay. Two experts on short wave length equipment will be in the party. No attempts will be made to broadcast over great distances. Communication with the world will be through relayed messages from the base ship to Green Harbour.

"Our expedition," Byrd said, "has been conservatively planned. We are not going to make any wild dishes to the Pole, but we hope to reach that objective if possible to do so. Our hope is to show that serial navigation of the Polar region is possible. With this accomplished, we will have made a great advance in the development of commercial aviation."

A wireless message from Point Barrow announces the safe arrival of Nielsen, the pilot of the Wilkins North Polar Expendition.

## [17] SUGAR RESTRICTION.

**CUBAN PARLIAMENT ADOPTS**

**HEAVY PENALTIES:**

*(Reuter's American Service.)*

Both Houses of Congress have approved of the Bill for the restriction of the sugar crop and the establishment of a sugar intelligence Committee.

The Bill provides a fine for every bag of sugar milled over each planter's quota and a fine of £2,000 a day on mills violating the decree fixing the date for the commencement of the sugar harvest.

## [23] PEKING COMMUNISTS

Peking, April 21.
The activities of Communists in Peking have been restricted to some extent. It is learned that they have decided to keep a few moderate members in Peking exclusively to enlist and train new members, and to send skillful workers to conduct propaganda among the employees of the Government railways. The majority of Communists leaving Peking will proceed to Chengchow, Kaifong, and Talyuanfu, the rest will travel along the Peking-Mukden and Tiantsin-Pukow lines with the exception of a few who will start work on the Peking-Sulyuan line. Most of the comrades now remaining in Peking have removed their residences in order to escape the attention of the police.—"Kuo Wen New Agency."
