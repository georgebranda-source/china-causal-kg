**AUSTRALIA'S VOICE**

### Melbourne Sept. 6.

## [24] QUEER CHOW.

**ARMADILXO SOLD HERE FOR FOOD.**

How an armadillo is prepared for food by local Chinese was related to a "China Mail" reporter to-day.

The animal was on sale in Taipo last month and then brought down by train to Yaumati.

Preparation of an armadillo for a meal is somewhat similar to that for a hedgehog in Europe.

The body is wrapped up in clay and then roasted. When this "cooking" is over, the clay is knocked off and the scales of the armadillo come off at the same time without any trouble, leaving the meat. The heat is brought to an extent whereby the entrails shriveled up into a small ball which can be easily removed.

Believing the animal to have considerable dietary benefit, some Chinese pay high prices for this delicacy.
