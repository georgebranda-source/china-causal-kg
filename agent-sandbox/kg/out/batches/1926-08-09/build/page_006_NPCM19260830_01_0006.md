## [11] ROYAL BIRTHDAY.

**EMPEROR YOSHIHITO OF JAPAN.**

**OBSERVANCE LATER:**

To-morrow is the birthday of the Emperor of Japan.

Celebrations are held later in the year on account of the heat (in Japan) at this time of the year.

The present Mikado, Yoshihito, was born at Tokyo in 1879. He succeeded his father on July 30, 1912, and was married on May 10, 1900.

On November 25, 1921, owing to the continued ill-health of the Emperor, the present Crown's prince (Hirohito) was constituted legent.

The Japanese claim that their empire was founded by the first Emperor, Jimmu Tenno, in B.C. 660, and that the dynasty founded by him still reigns.

By the Imperial House Law of 1889, the succession to the throne has been definitely fixed upon the male descendants.

The Hongkong Hockey Club will hold their annual meeting at 5.30 p.m. on Monday next in the lecture room, /Volunteer Headquarters.

## [13] LOCAL WEDDING.

**CEREMONY AT ST. JOHN'S CATHEDRAL**

**WHEELER—FOWLER.**

St. John's Cathedral was the scene of a pretty wedding on Saturday afternoon, the parties being Edith Mary Fowler, eldest daughter of Mr. and Mrs. G. Fowler, of the China Sugar Refinery, East Point; and Henry G. K. Wheeler, eldest son of Mr. and Mrs. G. Wheeler of Southampton, England.

The Rev. H. Copley Moyle conducted the service.

The bride, who entered the church on the arm of her father, by whom she was given away, wore an "very satin" dress, trimmed with silver face and orange blossoms with train and veil of white silk net and orange blossom wreath. She carried the bouquet of white roses and made denhair fern.

The Misses Annie Fowler (sister of the bride) and Babs Bliss, bridesmaids, wore, peach coloured crepe-de-chine dresses with gold shoes and hat to match, and carried bouquets of peach blooms and maidenhair.

The Misses Edna Stephens and Florrie Fowler (sister of the bride), train bearers, wore white satin and georgette petal frocks, satin slippers and silver tissue coronets, their bouquets being of white roses and maidenhair.

The bride's mother was attired in old rose georgette.

Mr. G. Flynn, of the Chinese Customs' Lappa, acted as best man, and Mr. M. J. Davidson, of the Chinese Customs, Kowloon, was groomsman.

## [19] DUTCH: HOLIDAY.

**QUEEN WILHELMINA'S BIRTHDAY.**

**CONSUL "AT HOME."**

Residents of Holland celebrate the birthday of their Queen Wilhelmina to-morrow.

Queen Wilhelmina Helena Pauline Maria was born on August 31, 1880, the daughter of King Willem III and his second wife, Princess Emma of Waldeck.

On the death of her father she succeeded to the Dutch throne on November 23, 1890. Coming of age, she was crowned on September 6, in 1898.

In 1901 she was married to Prince Henry of Mecklenburg-Schwerin.

A daughter, Princess Juliana was born in 1909.

The present royal family of the Netherlands is known as the House of Orange.

There are quite a number of Dutch institutions in Hongkong. The J.C.J.L. is a very important shipping office, there are a few business hongs, the Netherlands Harbour Works Co. (North Point and Macao), a social club, and a tennis club.

To-morrow the local Consult General will be at home at the Chancery, between 11 and noon.
