*(Reuter's Service.)*

**TALE OF A COOLIE'S PAY.**

Government is. keeping the 180,000 strikers who left Hong Kong for the Chinese City, where they are getting twenty cents a day and one meal, in case it has to increase the army owing to a revival of the strife with the Yunnanese, remarks "The 'Topicist' in the "Singapore Free Press." The Topicist sees No. 1 Boy of his own bachelor mess in the forefront of the battle, nasally warbling "The Minstrel Boy to the War has gone, And has left his pe-e-rks behind him."

A message from Cheklang states that a Chinese mob, yesterday, raided the Church Missionary Society's hospital at Hang-chow.

The raiders captured two male nurses, who were somewhat ill-treated. However, they were eventually rescued, without serious injury, by a party of Chinese Police who have now promised to protect the hospital.

## [13] TWO DEPARTMENTS IN ONE.

**AMERICAN FLEET LEAVES MELBOURNE.**

Hongkong generally has got its back up. Instead or bribing the house servants who went on strike because they were (or said they were) threatened, the idea is to pay them no wages at all for June, while absent, and reinstate them not on more favourable terms, but on worse. "That'll teach the varmints." The magistrate has held that a master who likes to prosecute a striker can secure judgment for a month's wages for leaving without notice. We hope he may get it.

## [15] SUN PAO-CHL

This reminds one of an experience in Singapore. A coolie was caught stealing, was convicted and sent to jail for a month. When he came out he demanded, in addition to the pay due to him, a month's wage in lieu of notice. Not to be done, the employer counter-claimed for a month's wages, also in lieu of notice, and the two cases came before the magistrate. Ex-prisoner coolie makes his statement, which wasn't exactly the whole truth, but was true in that his services were dispensed with. The employer was his own lawyer and the first question he asked was "Why did you leave?" "Jail." "Why?" "False case of stealing." "From whom?" "You." The magistrate's eye twinkled. Claim dismissed with costs. Counter-claim admitted, with costs. In default of payment, imprisonment for a fortnight. Wah!

## [17] JARDINE, MATHESON & CO., LTD.

[Sun Pao-chi has been a Cabinet Minister in Peking for some time.]
A communique from Rabbit states that extensive operations were undertaken by mobile columns, on August 2, in the north-western districts, where the enemy had recently penetrated in large numbers. The result was the ejection of the enemy from their stronghold in the Azjen Mountains. The enemy fled in disorder, leaving sixty dead.
Newspaper despatches, describing the operations, state that the French infantry fought most vigorously and tenniously despite the intense heat.

Hongkong, 29th July, 1925.
