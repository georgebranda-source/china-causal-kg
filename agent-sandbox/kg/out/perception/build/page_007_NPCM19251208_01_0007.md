He spent most of his time round the mountain of Santubong at the foot of which he had found a stone image, a carving of a woman on a sandstone rock.

It was discovered in 1886 by a Malay fisherman and represented the Great Mother. A striking feature was the peculiar form of headdress which might ultimately form the clue to the identity of the designers. Dr. Datt who had found in Africa what had been termed the "missing liak" had discovered similar images in Rhodesia.
