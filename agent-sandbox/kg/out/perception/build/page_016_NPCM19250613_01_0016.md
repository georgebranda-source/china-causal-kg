## [22] DAZED BY FUMES.

**IMPRISONED IN STRONG-ROOM.**

While working in a strong-room in a new building in Bath Street, Glasgow, two masons, James McGrory and Thomas Joyce, were imprisoned for a quarter of an hour by the heavy door of the room accidentally closing.

A coke fire had been placed in the room, which is in the basement, to dry the walls, and owing to the fumes from the fire the two men were in a dangerous position.
The door, weighing half a ton, being jammed with debris, the workmen outside were unable to effect a speedy rescue of their mates and had to break through the wall and the ventilator above the door so as to pass a crowbar to the imprisoned men.

When the door was forced open, McGrory and Joyce were in a dazed condition. They were taken to the Royal Infirmary and after treatment were allowed to go home.
