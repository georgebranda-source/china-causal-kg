## [11] THAT NEW PORT.

**LOWER THAN WHAMPOA FAVOURED? RIVER HEAL SCHEME**

“A certain gentleman who comes from Canton” is quoted by the vernacular papers of this morning, as having expounded a project by the Provincial Government to organise a big port at the mouth of the Canton River, further down than Whampoa.

At first, so the speaker is reported, the Kwangtung Government considered the Bak Ngo Taam (that part of the Canton Harbour by Shameen and leading to the "back reach") the widest anchorage. It was the intention to accommodate bigger ships at Whampoa but the water there has been found to be too shallow. Accordingly, a project is being mooted to develop the port of Tai Ping (at the head of the estuary and close to Bocca Tigris) and its suburb of Sha Kok (sometimes known as the Sha Kok Fort).

## [12] STRIKE EXAMINATION

The Canton Admiralty has despatched the "Kong Hon" to patrol the Bocca Tigris Passage to examine incoming foreign vessels. It having been reported to the Strike Executive that merchandise is being shipped to Canton from Shanghai under changed labels special examination pickets have been added.

## [15] BACK IN COLONY.

**A RETURNED BANISHEE'S DOINGS.**

**SENTENCES TO FIT THE CRIME.**

Attempting to extort money by posing as a police constable and returning from banishment were charges against a Chinese brought before Mr. R. E. Lindsell at the Central Magistracy this morning.

The evidence was that a man was arrested on Sunday on a charge of being concerned in a Triad Society. Defendant, representing himself to be a constable, visited the man's home the following day and demanded $6 from his wife, stating that he would put in a good word with the police on behalf of her husband. Defendant was asked to call again for the money. The next day the husband was released and on hearing from his wife about the visitor's demand a trap was laid with the assistance of the police. When arrested he was recognised as a banisher.

Defendant was sentenced to three months hard labour for posing as a constable, and one year's hard labour for returning from banishment. In addition he was ordered to receive 15 strokes of the birch.

**SERVANTS FAIL TO APPEAR.**
