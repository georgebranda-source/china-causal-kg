## [13] TRUE ECONOMY!

**VIEWS OF CHANCELLOR OF EXCHEQUER**

**DIPARTIMENTAL SACRIFICE**

*(Rauter's Service.)*

*Leeds, Jan. 20.*

“If the Government was supported in the economy scheme, which it has prepared, no addition to the burden of taxation will be necessary,” declared Mr. Winston (Lurchill, Chancellor of the Exchequer, speaking at the Leeds Chamber of Commerce, but if the economy scheme was pulled to pieces and whittled down, the bill must be paid).

He announced that when Parliament met an Economy Bill would be introduced covering the whole field of expenditure. Every Department would be asked to make sacrifices. He declared that the policy of true economy was not complete if it did, not provide for wise expenditure.

The Government's main constructive measure would be a great scheme of electrical development. "If this Island is to continue to support the present immense population it cannot afford to be behind any part of the world in scientific organisation and equipment. Science and goodwill are the only two horses strong enough to pull our waggon up the hill."

Mr. Churchill mentioned that the could subsidy would probably approximate £21,000,000; three-quarters off which would go in paying the wages of the miners.

## [17] STRIKE THREAT.

**FRENCH MINERS WANT MORE WAGES.**

**REFERENDUM TO BE TAKEN.**

*(Reuter's Scroace.)*

The National Council of the Federation of French Mine Workers has adopted a resolution declaring that unless wages are increased by January 21, to compensate for the increased cost of living, a referendum will be taken on the question of a general strike of French miners.

## [18] MORE FLOODS

**FIVE INCHES OF RAIN**

*(Reuter's Sorvice.)*

Unprecedented rains in the low-lying Portuguese territory between Umtall and Beira have resulted in serious floods, which have caused a complete suspension of train, muil, and telegraphic services between Rhodesia and Beira.

Many natives have been drowned. Others, took refuge in trees and have been brought to Beira by motor boats.

The average rainfall in the district yesterday was five inches.

## [19] "MERE BANDITRY."

**MURDERS OF BOLSHEVIST OFFICIALS.**

**MOSCOW'S VERSION.**

*(Reuter's Service.)*

*Moscow, January 20.*

A semi-official version of the murders in the Bobruisk district states that they were mere banditry and had no political significance.

[A Rign message of January 15 stated:—It is reported that much unrest prevails among the peasantry in various parts of Soviet Russia, particularly in the Ukraine and White Russia, where armed anti-Bolsheviks are active. Murders of over-zealous Communist officials are reported, chiefly in the Bobruisk district, where the militia and local authorities are alleged to have refused to act against the rebels, and are even co-operating with them.]
