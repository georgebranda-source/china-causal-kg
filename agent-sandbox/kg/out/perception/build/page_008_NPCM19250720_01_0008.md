## [17] RUBBER BOOM.

*(Reutor's Service.)*

The India Manufacturers Association, representing ninety-five per cent. of the British manufacturers of rubber articles, has issued a manifesto protesting against the continuance of the operation of the Stevenson scheme and the present form for the restriction of the exports of crude rubber from Malay and Ceylon, and demanding that the scheme be abolished or so drastically amended that it will provide quick releases as and when the price of rubber goes beyond the limits aimed at by the scheme.

*(Reuter's American Service.)*

At Mr. Coolidge's suggestion a delegation representing American rubber interests has interviewed the Secretary of State, Mr. Kellogg, and explained the unprecedented difficulties of the industry owing to the high cost of rubber, which is attributed to British restrictions though it is not alleged that there is unfair discrimination against America especially.

Mr. Kellogg promised to consider a "memorial left on behalf of the Rubber Association.

Warsaw, July 18.—After an exciting, Wild-West type of chase in motor cars through the main streets, the Police captured three Communists in the outskirts of the city. Two Police and one Communist were killed and fourteen pedestrians and the remaining two Communists wounded.—Reuter.

## [22] KOREA'S FLOODS. (Reuter's Service.)

<sub>section under [21] NOTICE TO CONSIGNEES.</sub>

*Tokyo, July 18.*

Press despatches from Korea carry news of a repetition of floods in the neighbourhood of Seoul owing to three days of rain. Railway traffic is suspended in Seoul and the water supply cut off. Many casualties are feared, while property damage is extensive.

Communications with Seoul are apparently completely severed. Reports as they come through state that the river rose some thirty-six feet in the suburb of Ryuzan, breaking down embankments and flooding everywhere. The City is without light, water and gas. Troops are assisting the rescue work which is rendered most difficult owing to the terrific rush of water. Seoul is completely isolated and without light. Refugees are occupy
