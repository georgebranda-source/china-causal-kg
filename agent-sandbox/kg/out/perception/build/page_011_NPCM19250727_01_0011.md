## [8] GINGER

London

Mr. George Bernard Shaw and Mr. Hilaire Belloc, the well-known essayist and historian, engaged in a debate for charity.

Discussing the future, Mr. Shaw roundly attacked the churches. The most incompetent men, he said, could be found in the Vatican, and the most competent on the executive of the Bubian Society. There were not enough saints to go round. The world possessed a small nucleus of saints. The others were tiresome, snobbish, and downright scoundrels.

"The world," he declared, "is tired of parson and priest. I would not care, twopence if ill the churches crumbled. Our souls would still go marching on."

## [10] NOT IRRECONCIALABLE

The Director-General of Public Health, Dr. Dick, says that the views of Dr. Mayo and Sir Thomas Horder are not irreconcilable. Modern civilisation has improved health in some ways and caused its deterioration in others. Diseases due to bad sanitation and lack of ventilation have decreased, but nervous diseases and excitability, due to the high pressure of modern life and the rush after pleasure, has increased. The use of stimulants and narcotics has also grown.

“The history of the world has shown that changing conditions tend to create other forms of disease,” said Dr. Dick. “To-day, for instance, cancer is on the increase, while tuberculosis is decreasing.”
