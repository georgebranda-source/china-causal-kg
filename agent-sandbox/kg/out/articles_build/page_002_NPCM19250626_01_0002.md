HOML LABOUR VIEWS (Router) London, Juno 25:---
The General Council of the Trade Union Congress on the National Executive of the Labour Party at a joint meeting in London passed a resolution declaring that disgraceful working conditions in Chinese mills are the immediate cause of the present disturbance in China. The resolution expresses the opinion that immediate steps should be taken to carry out in the Treaty Ports the recommendations of the Shanghai Child Labour Commission and to assist the Chinese Government to give general effect to their recent factory act and raise the general conditions of Chinese workers. The extracerritorial regime is provocative and dangerous and should be ended as soon as possible by an international conference on British initiative. The proposed conference on Chinese tariffs gives the opportunity for such initiative in respect of the Treaty Ports.

SHANGHAI STUDENT PLOT (Router) Shanghai, June 25:--- The Police yesterday obtained information of a widespread student plot to hire motor-cars and dash round the International Settlement bombing and shooting. The Police ordered garages not to hire cars except to well-known customers. The special constables were mobilised and posted at strategic points. Young Chinese were searched for arms and other precautions were taken. The shipping situation is becoming worse. British and Japanese ocean-going ships are avoiding Shanghai.

FRANCE PROTESTS (Router) Peking, Juno 25:--
The Fronch Minister has handed the Chinosé Foreign
Ministor a note protesting against the killing of
a French merchant at Canton on Tuesday and roserving the right to claim reparation. Twenty thousand students, workman and merchants paraded the streets carrying banners, some of which were inscribed "Declare war against England!" Chinese troops and police guarded foreign premises, also the entrancos to the Logation quarter, but there was no sign of any disturbances.
