Ball of $5,000 was estreated at the Central Magistracy this morning in the case in which Pun Pul-sang was charged with manslaughter. ## [12] BABY'S LIFE.

**CRIPPLED PORTER ON FOOTBOARD.**

The prompt action of a young railway porter, Bert Lucas, turned what might have been a domestic tragedy into a comedy at Victoria's Station (South-Eastern Section).

Just before the 8.3 p.m. train to Gillingham left No. 4 platform a woman with a girl, aged 12, and another child, aged 1, had been saying good-bye to relatives leaving by the train. The woman had left her baby in the carriage when the train suddenly started.

Her elder daughter's screamed out: "Grandfather is taking away the baby," and as the train gathered speed the woman in the compartment held the baby out of the carriage window over the platform.

Lucas, thinking that the frantic woman was about to drop the baby from the moving train, ran the length of two carriages, sprang upon the footboard, and worked his way along towards the compartment. He seized the baby from the woman, and, although he is a cripple, having been injured in a shunting accident, regained the platform without losing his balance or dropping the child.

The mother by this time was in a fainting condition, and Lucas held the child until she was restored. He refused to accept any reward, and, without waiting for the thanks of the family, said that he was only doing his duty and walked away.
