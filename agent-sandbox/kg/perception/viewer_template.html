Method. A local model (Qwen 3.5 9B, temperature 0, frozen prompt) lists the cause → effect claims each article states explicitly, with a word-for-word quote, and a second pass records the speaker. Denied links are skipped. Each cause is matched to the nearest category anchor by cosine similarity (qwen3-embedding:8b). It is assigned if cos ≥ θ and beats the runner-up by 0.02, and is "other" otherwise. Events are clusters of descriptions at cosine distance 0.35. No category is assigned by hand.

Notation. n = number of claims. B, C, O = British (the paper, British officials, other British), Chinese (including the Canton government and Strike Committee), other or unknown. Scope: broad = effect placed in Hongkong or Canton or unplaced; strict = Hongkong or Canton named. In B, an arrow is asserted (solid) when its claims share an article with the next link, and composed (dashed) when it joins different articles; composed chains are leads, not findings.

Limits. Speakers and categories are machine-assigned and not yet checked against a hand-coded sample. Counts are small, so read them as presence or absence. "Red agitators" versus "strikers are Red" is the least reliable split. Months after July 1925 cover only pages already OCRed.
