MORTGAGEE'S POSITION

The case was continued at the Summary Court this morning involving the position of a mortgagee who, in default of interest payment by the mortgagor, took over control of the premises in respect of which the mortgage was made only to find that rent in excess of standard rent had been paid to the mortgagor and that the tenants claimed to be entitled to refrain from paying further rent until the excess had been met.

Mak Tung-shi claims $1,000 (through her attorney, Ip Li-kong) from the National Optical Company, tenants of the premises in question, in respect of rent.

Mr. M. K. Lo is for plaintiffs and Mr. L. D. Turner for defendants.
