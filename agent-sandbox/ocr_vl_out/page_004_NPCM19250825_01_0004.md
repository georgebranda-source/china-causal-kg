## OBITUARY

### REV. FATHER ZAMPONI

The local Catholic Mission has lost one who worked in Hongkong and the surrounding inland area for a number of years by the death in Milan of the Rev. Father Zamponi. For a number of years he had been the Head of the West Point Orphanae in Hongkong and his work in the inland mission districts, especially. Hoifung, was singularly successful.

He left Hongkong for Milan as Vicariate of Hongkong on the Mission Council, and after serving for four years, he was appointed General Procurator of the Milan Foreign Mission Society in Rome. He returned to Holfung in 1920 but owing to indifferent health was obliged to return home where, however, he still continued his good work and until the last was spiritual director in the Milan Seminary. He was 56 years of age at the time of his death.

## MILITARY RITES

The military funeral held yesterday at Happy Valley Roman Catholic Cemetery was that of Gunner Bowden of the 13th Heavy Battery, stationed at Lyemun, who died as a result of septic poisoning and meningitis.

The coffin, placed on a gun carriage and headed by the band of the 2nd Battalion, East Surrey Regiment, was drawn to the Cemetery by the late gunner's comrades. Two officers and a number of men from all ranks of the R.G.A. followed the cortege and were present at the grave side.
