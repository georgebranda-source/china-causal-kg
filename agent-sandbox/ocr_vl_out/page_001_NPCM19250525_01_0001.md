## A NEW PROPELLER

SMART WORK BY KOWLOON DOCK

The Douglas Steamship Company's steamer Hai Ning arrived from Swatow on Saturday, having curtailed the usual stay at Foochow. She was promptly docked on arrival here to have her propeller replaced by a new one. The Kowloon Dock Company mustered all hands, with the result that nearly a record was put up. The old propeller was taken out, the new one put in, and yesterday the Hai Ning was taken on a sort of trial trip.

The Hai Ning steamed back to her accustomed place at the Douglas Steamship Company's wharf, and sails to-morrow afternoon for Swatow, Amoy, and Foochow.

The fact of the ship being “dressed” for Empire Day seemed to add a sense of undue importance to the occasion, the flags fluttering gaily in the breeze and making the “trial” something out of the ordinary for the fitting of a new propeller.
