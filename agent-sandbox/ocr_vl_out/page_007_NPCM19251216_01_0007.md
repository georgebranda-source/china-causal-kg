## GOOD FRIENDS

AUTONOMY

CHINESE MERCHANTS MEET.

Of the many items on the agenda of yesterday's monthly meeting of the committee of the Hongkong Chinese General Chamber of Commerce, one related to a suggestion by the Chinese Banking Exchange that the local Government be requested to make allotments under the Trade Loan more freely.

Mr. Li Yau-tsun (chairman) said that applications would now be considered more quickly.

A letter was read from the China National Chamber of Commerce. The meeting concurred with the sentiments expressed that the writers solely wanted tariff autonomy on a co-operation basis and not an indiscriminate increase of duty on imports, such increase possibly prejudicing foreign friendship.

The Chamber was also requested to broadcast telegrams denying malicious rumours in places where overseas Chinese resided, rumours having been circulated that banks in Hongkong had suspended payment.

## The Meeting

Proceeding on foot along a path on the West side of the hill there, he saw three Chinese running towards the frontier. Witness crouched down on the hillside and waited for them with a drawn revolver. When about 150 yards from him, the three men turned to the right, i.e., Easterward, and disappeared. Witness made for the ridge. When near the top he heard a shot from behind, and, turning, saw one of the three Chinese who thereupon fired a shot at him. Witness replied by discharging two rounds at the running man. His shots took no effect. The man disappeared into a gully behind a bank. Just then witness saw the other two men, who emerged from a hollow about 40 yards away.
