ATTACK BY MASKED MAN

Mrs. Doig, wife of a bank clerk, who lives near St. Albans, was brutally attacked by a man in her house and probably owes her life to her presence of mind, in feigning death. "After my husband had gone," she told a reporter, "a man whom I found in the hall clutched me by the throat and threw me to the ground. He belaboured me with a long cudgel, beating me about the head and body. In my arms I held Peter my little 14-month-old sop, and I expected him to be killed every second. The more I screamed the more the man hit me, so I pretended to be dead, lying perfectly still. A moment later the man stopped, and then I fainted and remembered nothing more till I found he had gone."
