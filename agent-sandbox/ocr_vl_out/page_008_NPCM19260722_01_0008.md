The Chire, a public Chire, and Nantao have a new job say the "N. C. Daily News." By the latest order of the Commissioner of Police, Gen. Yen Chun-yang, is their duty after midnight to chase "into bed" all coolies which may be found sleeping on the pavements. The order states that the Commissioner is feeling uneasy at the number of coolies in Chinese territory who turn the streets into beds in this low weather, such a practice, in addition to being against police regulations, being a menace to public health. Most of the beds it adds are laid after midnight. Whilst admiring the Commissioner's zeal for public health, we cannot help sympathising with the unfortunate coolies, for the average coolies quarters in a Chinese city are not the ideal summer resting place.

## SOCIAL AND PERSONAL NEWS

Tokyo, July 9.—The opinion is gaining ground that the Household expenses put at Y.4,500,000 a year shall be increased to Y.16,000,000. It is feared, however, that the proposal will not be realised in view of the financial difficulties confronted by the State at present. The expenses will, however, be increased by some Y.1,600,000, so that the total may come to approximately Y.6,100,000.

Washington, July 16—17: c.o. reimment of 150 more prohibition agents with an assignment to New York was ordered to-day by Acting Prohibition Chief Dow after a conference with Chester Mills, prohibition administrator of New York. Mr. Mills estimated that there are 15,000 places in New York where liquor is obtainable and that alcohol sales amount to from 400,000 to 500,000 gallons monthly. He said they would concentrate their forces on the sources of supply of alcohol and high powered beer.

His wife, son, and the maid perished in the flames. Dr. Paul Haupt, professor of Semitic languages, stated that Dr. Ember had been working for ten years on the book, which he had hoped to publish next year. When awakened by the fire, he rushed in his library, leaving his wife to rescue the child. Later, he was seen on the porch roof, clasping the manuscript, some sheets whereof have been recovered on the lawn and some more in the ruins of the library.
