## CANTON'S SURTAXES

### Peking, Oct. 14

Regarding Canton's proposed imposition of surtaxes on foreign goods, the Chinese (Peking) Government has come to conclusion that it is advisable for the Government at this juncture to express its views to the Foreign Powers, declaring the invalidity of the Canton Government's scheme on the ground that the latter has not gone through due formalities.
