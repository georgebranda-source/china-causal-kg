It seemed a pity that all games but one were postponed last week, it was evident that had some of the clubs waited a while before putting the game off, things would have looked better.

It would appear that a ruling from the Association re the postponing of fixtures seems necessary, at present there is no hard and fast rule on the matter, and some uniformity of action when necessary seems very advisable.

In my opinion no games should be cancelled or put off, before twelve o'clock on the day of playing, for it is a well-known fact that, as a rule, two hours of dry weather will put a green into playing order. without any chance of spoiling it.

## WAH YAN COLLEGE

NEW VOLUME OF THE DIAMOND MAGAZINE.

Printed by the Newspaper Enterprise, Limited, in both English and Chinese, "The Diamond," the magazine of the Wah Yan College has reached No. 1 of Volume II:

Besides the editorial by Mrs. A. Ethne Matthews, who succeeds Mr. Lim Hoy Lan, there are eleven articles and reports. These include "A Little Advice to Hongkong Chinese Students," Mr. Peter Tsun's Report for 1925"; and "The Late Mr. Lau Po."

## College Changes

Out of the 14 candidates presented for the Matriculation, 8 passed and 1 obtained the Senior Local Certificate.

The editorial states, in part:—In June, 1924, was started our school magazine under the able-editorship of Mr. Lim Hoy Lan, who we very much regret has had to vacate his honorary post owing to pressure of new duties as our Headmaster.

Chung Hok Nung obtained distinction in Physics and was awarded the Government Educational Scholarship. He is now in the Hongkong University and we wish him every success. Sung Shou Tin and Yung Kai Yin obtained distinctions in Trigonometry and History respectively. They were both offered the Hewitt Memorial Scholarship, but both were unable to accept it on account of conditions attached to it.
