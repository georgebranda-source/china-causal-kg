The question of a harbour survey being a topical one in view of yesterday's Council discussions and the assurance given by the Government, a "China Mail" representative made enquiries this morning as to the opinion in local shipping circles on the general question of harbour dredging.

A local resident with considerable shipping interests who was approached on the matter said that to him it seemed rather extraordinary in a port of such magnitude and importance as Hongkong, situated as it was at the mouth of a river, that the local Government had not a dredger of its own.

In the opinion of the gentleman in question there was bound to be silting in a port such as this and in spite of the assurance given in Council he was convinced that silting had had an effect on the shoal upon which two ships had recently grounded.

Hiring Difficulties.

Questioned as to the expense involved in the construction of a dredger, our informant said this would undoubtedly be very heavy but he did not see how else the situation was to be met. ## GUN RUNNING

BATTLE BETWEEN AEROPLANE

VESSEL

A SHOWER OF BOMBS.

A battle between a Government bombing aeroplane and a gun-running vessel endeavouring to land arms for the revolutionary forces is described in a report from Managua.

Two former American aviators, who are now employed with the Nicaraguan Constabulary, located a vessel off Corinto.
