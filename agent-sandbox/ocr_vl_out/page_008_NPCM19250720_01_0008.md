#### KOREA'S FLOODS. (Reuter's Service.)

## Tokyo, July 18

Press despatches from Korea carry news of a repetition of floods in the neighbourhood of Seoul owing to three days of rain. Railway traffic is suspended in Seoul and the water supply cut off. Many casualties are feared, while property damage is extensive.

Communications with Seoul are apparently completely severed. Reports as they come through state that the river rose some thirty-six feet in the suburb of Ryuzan, breaking down embankments and flooding everywhere. The City is without light, water and gas.
