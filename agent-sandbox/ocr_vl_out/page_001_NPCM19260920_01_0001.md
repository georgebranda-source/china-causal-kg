## NEW CLERGY

ORDINATION AT ST. JOHN'S CATHEDRAL.

CANDIDATES' TRIP.

The Bishop of Victoria (Rt. Rev. C. R. Duppy, D.D.) held an ordination service at St. John's Cathedral at the close of Matins yesterday morning, the candidates for the Ministry being the Rev. Wong Fook-ping and the Rev. Mo. Yuen-in who were ordained priests and Mr. Kong Chai-ming who was admitted a deacon.

Long before the appointed hour for the service a large congregation, mainly consisting of Chinese Christians, assembled in the Cathedral and by 11.30 it was found necessary to bring in a large number of additional chairs from the Cathedral Hall.

The procession, which formed up in the Cathedral compound was long and imposing, the combined choirs of St. Stephen's and St. Paul's Churches being followed by nearly twenty of the local clergy. Among those noted were the Revs. V.H. Copley Moyle, M.A. (Chaplain), C. I. Blanchett, M.A. (Secretary, C.M.S.), W. T. Featherstone (Headmaster, Diocesan Boys' School), A. D. Stewart, M.A. (St. Paul's College) and G. E. Arrowsmith, M.A. (St. Andrew's Kowloon).

The Bishop of Victoria, who was vested in his Convocation robes, followed, being attended by the Rev. N. V. Halward, M.A. (Diocesan Chaplain) bearing his Lordship's pastoral staff.

The candidates for ordination brought up the rear of the procession, accompanied by the "Rev. Wang Tang-ng of Canton", who presented them to the Bishop.

The “China Mail” understands that the three Chinese clergymen experienced something in the nature of an exciting time during the course of their journey from Canton, which was made by rail.

Considerable difficulty was experienced in obtaining the necessary permits in the first place and some opposition was met with when the party were stopped by a strike picket.

However, they managed to reach Hong Kong in safety and are hoping to arrange to leave for Canton in the course of the next few days.
