Ominous Rainstorms.

A message from Fez states that ominous rainstorms are falling in the Taza region which, if pressaging the beginning of the rainy season, are likely to delay the maturity of the French plans for a considerable time.

The Msoun river overflowed temporarily dislocating telegraphic communication between Fez and Taza.

At present the Riffs are trying to occupy the centre of the stage and are counter attacking at several points. Sharpshooters are harrassing the French garrison at Bibane.

The French claim that they have not only repulsed the attacks but have succeeded in advancing slightly at a point in the eastern sector.

Petain Takes Charge.

Marshal Petain is likely temporarily to assume the administrative in addition to the military direction of Morocco in succession to Marshal Lyautey whose resignation will be accepted by the Cabinet.

Abdel Krim's Threat.

A message from Fez states that rain stopped military movements being resumed by Abdel Krim. Reinforcing troops unsuccessfully attempted to occupy several posts.

Abdel Krim threatens dire vengeance to the tribes which have submitted to the French after having anticipatedly beaten the Spaniards.

CHINESE DELEGATION

ARRIVES.

(Reuter's Service.)

The Chinese delegation sent by Marshal Feng Yu-hsiung has arrived here to study political and economic conditions in the Soviet Republic.

KOWLOON ACCIDENT.



A Chinese girl was knocked down and injured at Cheung Sha Wan as a motor car belonging to the Chung Mei Garage was passing the Chiu Ki shipyard.

The driver of the car in his statement to the Police said that two girls suddenly darted in front of his car, and before he could pull up one of them was struck on the face by the mudguard. He picked up the injured girl and took her in his car to the Kwong Wah Hospital, where she was detained.

## HOCKEY

CLUB'S TRIAL MATCH.

Yesterday afternoon at the Marina Ground, Kowloon, a trial Hockey match was played between two teams of the Hongkong Hockey Club.

The Match was fairly well contested, although "Whites" captained by J. Lammert had the lead throughout the whole game, and eventually won by three goals to one. The late start made play towards the end very difficult owing to approaching darkness.

The match started with Whites pressing and after ten minutes' play, from a good forward movement Faers opened the score. The game then settled down to a very evenly contested match, neither side adding any further score before halftime. The second half opened with a brilliant rush by Vallack and Lammert, the latter netting, giving Whites an additional lead. This woke Colours up and they pressed hard with the result that they scored. Lammert, however, once more got going and put through another goal five minutes later. Lammert had the misfortune to lose a fine opportunity just before the end when after a fine run down, owing to the failing light, he just missed an open goal. The whistle blew shortly after.
