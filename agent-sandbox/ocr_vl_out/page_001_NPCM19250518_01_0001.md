## LISTENING IN

## Application Made to the Government

A scheme is at present before the Government by which the owner of the smallest wireless set in Hongkong may "listen in" to music, lectures, etc.

The “China Mail” announced some weeks ago that the Government had decided that it could not be directly responsible for any system of broadcasting in Hong Kong and indicated that it was up to local radio enthusiasts to make their arrangements accordingly.

Shortly after this announcement a meeting of the Radio Society was held at which it was understood that efforts were to be made to fit up a transmitting set. These efforts, the "China Mail" understands, have not borne fruit yet, owing to the difficulty of getting a licence from the Government, whom it would appear is anxious to have a guarantee of more or less permanency in connection with such a service before it feels disposed to grant a licence.

Meanwhile the Y.M.C.A. have submitted a scheme and made application for a licence in connection with a scheme for broadcasting lectures in Chinese and English, also suitable music, many of their supporters having advocated the provision of some such facilities. The Greater China Radio Co. are prepared to guarantee the provision of a Westinghouse transmitting set at the Y.M.C.A. headquarters and the scheme is likely to develop if the Government will grant the licence which has been asked for.

In connection with this latter particular, which has been the stumbling block in connection with the scheme of the Radio Society, a “China Mail” representative who made enquiries was informed that there would be no objection provided that regulations were complied with. The regulations are now being prepared.
