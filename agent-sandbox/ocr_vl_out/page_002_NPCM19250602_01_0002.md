The wages of some of these black workers, judged from the standard of a white man's needs, is often incredibly low, and so long as this condition exists the white population cannot sensibly increase.



There can be no objection to the black man doing the work for which his capacities are suitable. No British democrat would ever think of erecting a barrier against the development of the African native; but South Africa cannot have it both ways. She cannot at the same time have a satisfactory increase in the number of her white residents, and pay for her work at a price on which the white man cannot live.

The white man's function in South Africa at present is to act as trader, employer, teacher, or director of native labour. The unskilled and semi-skilled work available for the white worker in other lands is done in Africa by the native at a scale which is several times lower than the white man's meanest standard of existence, South Africa from end to end appears for more white people, but her practices is to fill her factories and all her undertakings with natives, and to pay them a wage which keeps the white worker out of Africa as effectively as if he were not allowed to go there.

Your arrival is being eagerly anticipated by the people of the Union, and we are hoping that the welcome awaiting your Royal Highness will make the memories of your tour delightful both to you and to us.

The whole subject requires careful and enlightened examination, and I offer nothing more than a simple and obvious suggestion. If South Africa is to be regarded as a white man's country, wages must be fixed upon the basis of the white man's needs, and if a native performs a white man's task he should be paid for it a white man's price. If that were done the white population would rapidly increase, and European civilisation in South Africa would be saved.

Migrations of birds, locusts, and ishes are well known, but few people are aware that such fruit insects are butterflies make extended migratory flights, often in enormous numbers and over hundreds and even thousands of miles. Mr. O. B. Williams, acting chief entomologist of the Egyptian Ministry of Agriculture, has been studying the flight of the Painted Lady, butterfly and gives an account of his researches in "Nature."

The insects originate, he thinks, somewhere in the "south or southeast of the long line of desert stretching across North Africa and Asia. Minor, between 2,000 and 3,000 miles from Southern England. Leaving the desert in the early spring, they arrive at the southern shores of the Mediterranean usually about April. From Pulestine they appear to fly through Syria and Turkey to the Balkan States, and from Egypt, Tripoli, and Algeria they cross to Southern Europe. All the time stragglers are dropping out or being left behind.

England is reached at the end of May or beginning of June, Scotland a week or two later, and they have even been recorded in Iceland—a total flight of about 4,000 miles—in July. Other drifts follow at intervals throughout the summer. Whether in crossing the sea they continue flying through the night or rest on the water is not clear; nor is it known if a return journey is made, although it is certain that if there is a return flight it must be in a later generation.
