One of the attractions in Japan is the Tokyo fish market. It is the longest in the country and occupies 480,000 square feet. The market is an association of 750 wholesalers and 552 brokers who operate under the supervision of municipal authorities. Two thousand fish mongers and buyers from numerous hotels and restaurants assemble at this market every morning between 5 and 10 o'clock and carry away with them from 800 to 1,200 tons of fish.

A report that the personal bodyguard of the Prince of Wales has been doubled, and is now under the command of a chief inspector of the Special Branch of Scotland Yard, has been officially denied. An official of the Prince's household told a representative of "The Daily Chronicle" that the Special Branch inspector who for years has accompanied the Prince has recently been on short leave, and that a temporary arrangement had been made for other officers to perform his duties.

An influential delegation, representing 300 chambers of commerce: and several important national trade organisations visited Winston Churchill, Chancellor of the Exchequer to make a vigorous protest against the present tax methods by which business is done by co-operative societies, which receive more favourable tax treatment than private trading. France has successfully prohibited absinthe as a beverage and accomplished it in a single day. The absinthe habit was rooted in the French people. Up to the first days of the war the Paris boulevard and all France had its "hour of absinthe" or its "green hour" every afternoon at 5 o'clock.
