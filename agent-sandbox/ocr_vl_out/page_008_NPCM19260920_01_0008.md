Mr. J. Nienhuys, one of the founders of tobacco-growing in Deli, Sumatra, recently celebrated his ninetieth birthday. This pioneer of one of the most flourishing cultures in the Dutch East Indies, started for the D.E.I. at the age of twenty-three. Native labour was proved a disappointment, and success only came when he recruited Chinese in Penang. In 1869, the Deli Co. was founded. Mr. Nienhuys personally directed affairs in Deli until 1871, when he returned to Holland. He is still, however, a Director of the Deli Co.
