# LOCAL AND GENERAL.

Silk forwarded from Hongkong by the Empress of Canada on May 15 arrived in New York (St. John's Park) on the 6th inst., having been 22 days in transit.

The return of notifiable diseases for the 48 hours ended Sunday shows one case of paratyphoid and two cases of cerebrospinal fever—all Chinese.

Eels to growing quantities are being taken from the coastal and inland waters of the Canadian Dominion each year. During the 1923 season 1,114 cwt were caught by sea fishermen and 13,753 wertaken in inland waters, as compared with 1,434 cwt and 11,710 cwt., respectively, during the previous year.

The port authorities at Melbourne were puzzled recently by the unexpected appearance of the strangest sailing craft they had ever seen. "She had four schooner-rigged musts, but the rear one seemed to have been snapped, off near the top," said it was thought she might have put into port to refit. "The vessel turned out to be the Danish auxiliary schooner Danekonge (1,688 tons gross), en route from Auckland to Port Pire, which put into Melbourne for oil supplies and engine repairs. One of her propellers was disabled. The Danekonge was built in 1920, and is one of the few ships of her type. She is one of the most economically run cargo carriers afflated. Her main means of propulsion are four huge schooner sails, but two auxiliary Diesel engines of 160 h.p. each have been installed for use in calms and head winds.

There are between fifty and sixty thousand Hungarian (Magyar) immigrants and their children in Canada, according to Dr. Frank Hoffman of St. Andrew's College, there being settlements at Otthon, Punnichy and Regina composed of very industrious, contented people with large families. In 1921 Dr. Hoffman established a literary society at Regina, from which important social and national work among the Hungarians is carried on and the ideals of Canadian citizenship brought constantly before them. Many have been very successful on the land, among whom are J. Troth, Sr., who, beginning with a capital of but $200 now, with his five sons, owns twenty quarter sections of land.

Mrs. Brunson has been very popular in Manila society during her stay of three years in the Islands.
