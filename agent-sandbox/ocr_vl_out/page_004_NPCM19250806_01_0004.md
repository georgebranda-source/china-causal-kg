## Hongkong, Thursday, August 6, 1925

## MOMENTOUS MATTERS

All eyes now, and for some time to come, should be on Shanghai. We must remember that if there had been no outbreak at Shanghai, there would probably have been no trouble—or the Canton "Government" would have had no immediate cause to provoke trouble with a place that has been consistently friendly with the Cantonese and has, at various times, proved a safe harbourage for absconding officials, some of whom have not been courageous enough to face music of which they have called the tune. The enquiry into the Shanghai outbreak will, we hope, lead to a satisfactory peace. If an unconscionable time has been taken in fixing up preliminaries, we must reconcile ourselves to the fact that it is the way of diplomacy to be slow, and that with a people prone to be slow themselves, caution is the first necessity. Hongkong shot to be deeply interested in the coming enquiry. It is likely that the Colony will be represented on the enquiry and it is

ability its representative will be the Chief Justice, Sir Henry Gollan. This would be an excellent appointment. The thing demands the calm and legal mind, able to sift evidence and to weigh it. But Hongkong's interest is beyond the question of a local appointment. Given a " satisfactory issue out of the enquiry, Canton has nothing to fall back upon in the way of excuses for encouraging strikers, fostering hatred, and ruining itself into the bargain. The result of the enquiry suggests another aspect. It suggests the reunification of the different and warring Chinese elements, who in their own antagonisms have done so much to place China where she is at the moment—the wilful child in the family of nations. It suggests China sober instead of China drunk. It suggests China developing her incomparable and vast natural resources; a competitor in the world's trade rightly demanding and meeting, respect; contributing not merely to the trade of the world, but to its arts and sciences. It is this China that foreigners desire to meet, to which end such institutions as the Hongkong University stand. The picture may suggest a measure of hyper-optimism; but without optimism there is no hope in Hongkong or China for anyone. Officials from Canton brave the journey to Peking. In ordinary circumstances a visit to Peking by a Cantonese official would be fraught with the gravest consequences—to his head! All this is altered. Factions and fractions can meet on what they believe is common ground—to resist the alleged intrusions of the perinacious foreigner. If they can be led to believe that the best weapon with which to resist, or meet the foreigner, is that of copying his trade methods—putting it on a material basis only—that will be great rain. It will be a great rain for Hongkong.

## Once Again

Revolutions in Greece have become the veriest commonplace. As often as not almost Government succeeds Government in that unhappy country not by the constitutionally expressed wish of the people, but as the result of a coup d'etat. During mail week Greece experienced her seventh revolution in the space of 21 years. On this occasion naval and military leaders, in the persons of General Pangalos and Admiral Hadji Kyriakos, backed by the sailors and soldiers, simply walked in and took possession of the Government departments. For once, happily, there was no bloodshed. The usurpers, having made themselves masters of the situation by the mere demonstration of force, announced their willingness to approve of a Cabinet in which the two leaders of the revolt should hold respectively the portfolios of the Army and Navy—in other words, be virtual dictators. Whether Greece would be better off under a Dictatorship as in the case of Italy, or a Directorate, as in the case of Spain, it is not easy to say. What can be said is that, since the downfall of Venizelos, there has not been a more sadly misgoverned country in Europe—Russia hardly excepted—than Greece, and none which has become a more negligible factor in European councils. "Tis Greece, but living Greece no more."
