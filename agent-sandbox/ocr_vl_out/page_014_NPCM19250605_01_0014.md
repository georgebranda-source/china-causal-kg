## FREAK DINNER

## ELEPHANTS AND CAMELS IN BALLROOM

One of those freak dinners by which American millionaries love to display their capacity for “hanging the expense,” has taken place at the Waldorf Astoria, New York.

As the guests entered they suddenly found themselves lost in a grove of palm trees with monkeys swinging on the branches.

Arab guides received the guests and conducted them to a large ballroom arranged to imitate the desert.

Sand four inches deep covered the floor and appropriate scenery hid the walls.

Two hundred guests were served by waiters, genuine and imitation natives, but all were in costume. During the dinner dervishes, sword jugglers and dances performed on the sand. Many guests were alarmed for a moment when Arabs on horseback dashed in and gave their performance.

The finale was a procession of elephants, camels and other animals. This was somewhat spoiled by the behaviour of the camels. As soon as they smelled the sand they rushed in snorting, lunging about, and finally threw themselves down and rolled delightedly on the floor.

Mathew Brush, the banker host, was delighted with the success of his effort. Incidentally, he is a large shareholder in the hotel, which naturally will not lack publicity.
