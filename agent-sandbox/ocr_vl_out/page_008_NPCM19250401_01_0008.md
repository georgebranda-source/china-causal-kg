## CHINA POLITICS

PROVINCIAL FÉDÉRATION SYSTEM

(Courtesy of the Daily Bulletin.)

Peking, March 31.

Tuan Chi-jui telegraphed the following reply to the recent communication from Tang Chi-yao and Chao Heng-ti, advocating the adoption of the provincial federation system:—

“I quite appreciate your proposal for the adoption of the provincial federation system. However, the chief object of the recent political reconstruction efforts has been to enable the aims of the national and provincial constitutions to be realised simultaneously. When I arrived in the capital on November 1, I issued several circular telegrams announcing this, and at the inauguration of the Rehabilitation Conference, I emphatically stated that the adoption of a Provincial Constitution must be encouraged. I think this does not differ greatly from the view you have expressed. Our fellow-countrymen, therefore, must hope that whatever organisation is to draft the new Constitution for the nation will speedily come into being, so as the reconstruction efforts now being made may achieve success. I, however, have referred your telegram to the Conference.”

The Reorganisation Conference will meet again to-day after a fortnight's recess.

Fifteen different items are on the agenda, mostly referring to matters connected with military reorganisation, abolition of the Tuchun system and similar questions.

After an animated discussion it was decided to refer all the fifteen items to a committee of military experts, for investigation and to report within five days.

## DIRECT CABLES

GERMAN AND AMERICAN

AGREEMENT.

(Reuter's Service.)

Berlin, March 31.

An agreement has been reached between the German and American cable interests for the re-establishment of direct communication between the two countries.

The German company will handle the entire traffic of the Commercial Western Union Companies to and from Germany between Emden and the Azores while the American companies will administer the traffic from the Azores to America. Portugal has agreed to the landing of the cable at the Azores.
