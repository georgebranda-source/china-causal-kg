## PERJURY CHARGE

Lau Hey-chi, of No. 1, Bonham Road, merchant, was charged before Mr. R.A.D. Forrest at the Central Magistracy yesterday with perjury. The case arose out of the evidence tendered by defendant in a case in which a Chinese was charged with theft of a blanket and acquitted after the evidence of the defendant's mother. The defendant then stated that his mother was absent in the country, which proved to be untrue.

Mr. C. A. S. Russ, defending, submitted that perjury is only a criminal offence. If it is made deliberately and not accidentally. As far as the defendant was concerned, he acted on the belief that his mother had gone to the country as arranged and, not having been home the might previous to the case, he was not aware that she had not gone away.

The defendant was discharged fter evidence given by him and is mother.
