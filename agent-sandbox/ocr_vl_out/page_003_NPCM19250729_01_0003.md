## CREW SAVED

S. S. EGREMONT CASTLE FOUNDERS.

### (Router's Service.)

Manila, July 28.

The British steamer Egrmont Castle, of the Dodwell Castle Line, which went aground last week on Tub Butahu reef in the Sulu Sea, slipped off the reef in a squall and sunk in a thousand fathoms on Sunday morning.

The crew of fifty were saved by the Coastguard cutter Corregidor and now are on their way to Manila. A valuable cargo of sugar was lost. The automatic light on the reef was unlit when the vessel grounded.

The Coastguard, utter Corregidor has arrived with 12 white and 86 Chinese, comprising the entire crew of the Egremonv Castle. They state the ship was slipping off the reef but had not yet sink when they left the scene. They believe, however, she is lost. They declared that the transfer from the vessel was made with difficulty owing to high seas and heavy wind.

[Messrs. Dodwell] and Co., Ltd. agents here for the Castle Line have received confirmatory advice.

## RIFF WAR

SPANISH AEROPLANES ACTIVE.

(Reuter's Service.)

Mudrid, July 28.

A communiqué from Morocco says that a French aeroplane traversed Quilates, in the Spanish zone, while Spanish aeroplanes bombed enemy concentrations advancing on the French front near Hashuegan. The French and Spanish air forces have thus begun to exercise the right to fly over each other's territory, as agreed at Madrid.

## PEACE - COMING?

(Reuter's Service.)

Paris, July 28.

It is reported from Fez that signs of lossitude have appeared amongst many Riff tribes, several of whom are demanding a cessation of hostilities.

The death is announced of Dr. Stafford Moritz Cox (Major R.A.M.C.), M.D., L.R.C.S., L.R.C.P.I., which occurred on July 24, at the General Hospital, Shanghai, in his 60th year. Dr. Cox, who was a native of Kerry, Ireland, had been ill for the past three or four years, but lately had shown great improvement, and his death came as a great surprise.

## TSAO KUN FLEES

SHAVES MOUSTACHE AND FAILS.

(Router's Service.)

Peking, July 28.

According to Chinese reports, ex-President Tsao Kun, who has been a prisoner since Peng Yu-hsiang's coup d'etat, attempted to escape on July 24, disguised as a common soldier, with his moustache shaved off, but the sentry recognised him and called assistance and Tsao Kun was led back into captivity.
