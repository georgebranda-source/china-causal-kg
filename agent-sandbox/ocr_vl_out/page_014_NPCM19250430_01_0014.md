## EROS IN PINK TIGHTS

An amusing rag by students took place in Cambridge on March 9 in connection with collection on behalf of the first pickers in Wisbech district.

A pyramid 30ft. high had been erected in the centre of the square with the apex veiled. King Henry VIII, attended by Cardinal Wolsey, pulled a cord which released the wrappings, and there poised on one foot was Eros.

Sitting at the foot of the monument were real flower sellers imported from Piccadilly for the occasion. They did not recognise their Eros, for the famous statue was being impersonated by the heaviest man in the University, who is well known as a weight putter. He was dressed in pink tights and red bathing costume, with wings complete. It was bitterly cold, and to add to his discomfort oranges were rained at him from all angles. As compensation, somebody climbed the pyramid with a bottle of beer.



At one period there was a formidable fray among the students. Oranges, tomatoes, apples, eggs, and bags of flour flew in all directions.
