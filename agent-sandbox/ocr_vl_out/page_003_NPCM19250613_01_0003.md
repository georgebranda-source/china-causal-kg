ROLL SYSTEM AGAIN.



PAYMENT FOR USE OF "ROAD-DE-LUXE."

The scheme for the construction of a special road from London to Brighton, to be used by motor vehicles only, has so far advanced that a company for carrying it out, London and South Coast Motorways, has been registered, and a Bill to obtain the necessary Parliamentary sanction, backed by members of all parties, is to be introduced in the House of Commons.

The proposed motorway will start at a junction with the Kingston by-pass road, 60 ft. wide, at present under construction by the Surrey County Council. This bypass road turns off from the Portsmouth. Read just beyond the bridge over Beverlybrook, in Purney Vale, near the Robin Hood Gate entrance to Richmond Park, and, running parallel, with the brook ultimately gets to Tolworth, where the motorway begins. From this point the route adopted passes between Epsom and Leatherhead, and via Pabble Coombe, Buckland, Horsham, and Henfield, to a terminus at Albourne, near Pycombe, whence existing roads afford direct communication with Brighton, Hove, and Worthing. The Sussex County Council proposes to construct a by-pass road from Albourne to Brighton, similar to the Kingston by-pass.

Forty miles will be the approximate length of the motorway. It will be fenced, as in the case of a railway, provided with entrances and exits at points on neighbouring existing roads, and in the case of cross-roads will pass under bridges, the construction of which, for outside traffic, is part of the scheme. There is no need for toll gates at the entrances and exits. Every vehicle pays on entering the motorway and gets a ticket which it gives up on leaving the motorway. The carriageway will be 50 ft. wide with a concrete surface capable of accommodating five lines of traffic, allowing the speedy car and the slower lorry to travel at their own speeds. It is proposed that the tolls should be three-farthings per mile for motorcar; a farthing per mile for motorcycles; and a farthing per ton mile for commercial vehicles. It is thought that the motorway will take two years to construct, and the estimated cost is £2,580,000.
