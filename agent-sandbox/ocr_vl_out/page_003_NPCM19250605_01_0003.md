## SIR EYRE CROWE

The death took place at Swanage of Sir Eyre Crowe, Permanent Under-Secretary of State for Foreign Affairs since 1920, aged 61, after 40 years' service. He was born at Leipzig, where his father, Sir Joseph Archer Crowe, was Consul-General, and he was educated at German schools. His mother was a German, and these circumstances, and the fact that he married a German, led to unjust criticism against his being Assistant Under-Secretary during the war.

AIRCRAFT LIGHTHOUSE.



MOST POWERFUL IN THE WORLD.

The most powerful lighthouse in the world has just been inaugurated by France near Dijon. Its utility is not affected by the fact that it is nearly 300 miles from the nearest coast, since it is intended as a guide not for ships, but for aircraft flying on the great international air routes, such as Paris-Italy, Paris, Switzerland, and Paris Algeria. The lighthouse, built on the summit of Mont Afrique, is lit by one milliard candle-power—that is more than thirty times the power of Griżnez and Ushant lighthouses—and in clear weather its double beam, flashing at intervals of five seconds, will be visible over a radius of 800 miles. Even in dull weather it will carry for nearly 125 miles. This construction of this, the first real lighthouse to aircraft marks the last point in a world scene for many years.
