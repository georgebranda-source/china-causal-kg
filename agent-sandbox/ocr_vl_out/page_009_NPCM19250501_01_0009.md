## SHANGHAI SENSATION

## EXCITING RUNNING GUN-FIGHT

### (Reuter's Service.)

A Chinese kidnapper was killed, a companion was seriously wounded, two by-standers were slightly wounded and a foreigner narrowly escaped death in a running gun fight yesterday, when a Sikh constable detected a gang of kidnappers attempting to steal a 12-year old son of a wealthy Chinese, Sung Woo-pah.

Young Sung, who was in a ricsha, was stopped by a gang of six fully-armed men, two of whom detained the coolie, while the remainder made off with the ricsha and its occupant.

The coolie broke away and pursued the gang, raising the alarm, which the Sikh heard.

Seeing the gang coming towards him, one waving a pistol, the constable opened fire, which the bandits returned.

Many shots were fired. A stray one pierced the back curtain of a motor car driven by a foreigner.

The ricsha coolie was shot in the arm and another ricsha coolie received a small bullet wound in the wrist.

## MISSING WALLET

## POSTAL CLERK'S PROMPT ACTION

LUCKY: NAVAL MAN.

The prompt action of a clerk at the weighing $ _{0} $ counter of the G.P.O. parcels department saved a naval man from the loss of his wallet, which, amongst other securities contained a 100 note.

The incident occurred during a rush hour shortly before the tiffin interval to-day when a number of Chinese had gathered at the counter. Noticing certain individuals from this group casting suspicious eyes on the article which the naval man had in a moment of forgetfulness left behind, the clerk took possession of it and handed it to the Parcels Superintendent.

The owner of the wallet turned up an hour later, although, as might be imagined, not with any anticipations of recovering the lost article. A smile passed over the face of the naval man when the Superintendent told him that he had the wallet, which he promptly returned intact. The former expressed his thanks to the official and asked him to convey his gratitude to the clerk when he returned after tiffin, the message being accompanied with a gift of a five dollar note to the clerk for his prompt and honest act.
