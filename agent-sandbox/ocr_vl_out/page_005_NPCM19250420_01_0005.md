## OREGON ORIENTAL LINE

## SPORTS MEETING

The second annual athletic sports of the Sacred Heart English School were held at Happy Valley yesterday in the presence of a large gathering.

The programme consisted of over 30 items, all of which were keenly contested and in many of them some close finishes were seen.

The weather was a trifle hot for a sports meeting, and consequently the runners especially in the long distance events, had a strenuous time. A new event hitherto not included in a school sports programme was the candle race which provided some excitement, a possible winner having the light blown out almost near the winning post. The relay race open to all schools was won in excellent style by a team from St. Joseph's College.

The Headmaster (Mr. J. S. Shak) welcomed Mrs. Kotewall in a few words, after which the Hon Mr. Kotewall addressed the gathering. He said: "My wife has asked me to say a few words on her behalf before she gives away the prizes. I am sorry that I cannot speak up to-day, because I am suffering from what one of my doctors calls "smoker's throat," though my lips have never made the acquaintance of so much as a cigarette stump. Another doctor calls it a "clergyman's throat," though I have never been given to preaching, which I leave to my good lady. (Laughter).
