## BASEBALL PRACTICE

SATURDAY'S INTERESTING

Play was very interesting, the Club bringing out several new players of promise.

South China started with a blank first inning and the Club responded with 3 runs, Dome making the only home run of the match. In their fourth inning, the Club scored another run and the game finished with the score, Club 5 runs, S.C.A.A., 3 runs.

Shim, South China's star pitcher, gave way to Pan. Father Drought pitched three innings for the Club and Werschkul did-duty for the rest of the game. Both sides had errors in fielding.

Last Monday, the Shanghai Rowing Club's programme at Henli was shortened owing to the Volunteers being called up and the baseball match between the Japanese Olympiad team and Shanghai was cancelled because of the trouble.

## BOWLS LEAGUE

[By "Onlooker"]

There were some very surprising results in the bowls games on Saturday.

In the First Division, playing on their own ground the K.B.G. disposed of the Taikoo team by thirty points. At the Dock Club ground Craigengower gained an unexpected victory over the home team by sixteen points. The Civil Service again proved that they are a team to be reckoned with, for playing on their own ground they secured an overwhelming victory over the Kowloon Cricket Club by fifty points.

The results in the second Division did not contain any surprises. Taikoo just managed to win against the Indian Recreation Club by one point, although the latter were leading by five at the eighteenth head. The Police continued their sequence of victories, and the K.C.C. second string secured the points at the expense of the East Point Club.
